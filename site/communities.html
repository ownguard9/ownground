Communities

Every community owns its assets together, governs through its own DAO, and publishes its full financial history onchain.

The contract is not deployed yet. Communities will appear here once it is live on testnet.

Create a community

First create a Sputnik DAO for the community, then register it here. Membership is fixed at creation and cannot change later. The founding policies below are set at creation; later changes go through a two-step amendment (propose, wait the timelock, execute). Creating a community attaches 0.1 NEAR for storage.
