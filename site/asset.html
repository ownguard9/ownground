Value and revenue

Acquisition value, treasury balance and realised revenue are separate figures, never combined into one total.

Revenue

Record revenue

Onchain revenue attaches the amount plus 0.01 NEAR for storage and is verified at once. Reported revenue attaches 0.01 NEAR only, earns no points, and is labelled "Reported — not independently verified" until a different member attests it with a 0.1 NEAR bond. Every entry needs an evidence reference, and a reference cannot be used twice for this asset.

Expenses

Pay an expense

DAO only. The locked funds guard runs first, then the spending policy if the community set one. Attaches 0.01 NEAR for storage.

Spending policy: none set yet

Distributions

Run a distribution

Pays members from this asset's realised revenue under the approved policy. The activity slice uses only the current epoch's verified work points; after the distribution the epoch advances and the points reset. A distribution only pays out new revenue since the last one; a second call with no new revenue is rejected. Attaches 0.01 NEAR for storage.
