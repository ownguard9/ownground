    Governance

    All spending is approved by the community DAO. The buttons below create DAO proposals from your wallet; council members vote on them, and the DAO executes the approved call. Proposals live on the DAO itself.

    Open the DAO proposals

    Propose a distribution policy change

    Shares must sum to 100. The activity share is a slice of the member pool, split by verified work points instead of ownership. A change is proposed here, waits out the timelock, and then anyone can execute it. The old policy stays in force until then.

    Current policy: none set yet

    Propose a spending policy change

    The DAO sets a cap on single expenses, a minimum treasury reserve and the allowed categories. An expense outside the policy is rejected before any transfer, even a DAO approved one. Acquisitions are not capped. Changes go through the same propose, wait, execute flow.

    Current spending policy: none set yet
