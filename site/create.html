Create a community

The site creates a Sputnik DAO for the community, then registers it here. You can also use a DAO you already have. Membership is fixed at creation and cannot change later. The founding policies below are set at creation; later changes go through an amendment (propose, wait the timelock, execute). Creating a community attaches 0.1 NEAR for storage.

DAO for this community

Creating the DAO attaches 10 NEAR to the new DAO. That NEAR becomes the DAO's own balance for its own storage. It is not a fee. The DAO council is the members listed below, and 2 of them must approve a proposal for it to pass.
