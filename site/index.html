Own Together. Build Together. Grow Together.

OwnGround is the infrastructure for communities that own productive assets together. A group pools money, buys an asset through a vote, earns revenue from it, and shares the earnings under rules nobody can quietly change.

The problem

Savings groups pool money, but nobody can verify how it is used, who owns what, or where the revenue went.

The answer

Ownership, revenue and payouts live onchain, governed by the community DAO. A spreadsheet or an admin can be edited. A OwnGround community cannot.

The proof

Every step is a real testnet transaction you can check on the explorer. No simulated success, no fake balances.

How it works

  1. Create a community. Name it, name its DAO, name its members. Membership is fixed at creation.
  2. Fund an asset. Members contribute to a campaign with a target. Nothing is spent before the target is reached.
  3. Vote to acquire. The DAO proposes the purchase, names the seller, and votes. The contract pays the seller in the same call.
  4. Earn revenue. Income from the asset is recorded onchain. Real deposits are verified income; reported income is labelled as reported, never as verified.
  5. Pay expenses. Operating costs are paid from the treasury, only with DAO approval, and never from locked campaign funds.
  6. Approve a policy. The DAO sets the split: members, treasury retention, maintenance.
  7. Distribute. The engine calculates entitlements and pays members real NEAR. The split sums exactly to the distributable amount, every time.

The demo: Lagos Energy Cooperative

Four members pool 10 NEAR to buy a commercial generator. The generator earns 3 NEAR of rental income, pays 0.5 NEAR of fuel, and the remaining 2.5 NEAR is distributed 60 / 25 / 15: members receive 1.5 NEAR, the treasury keeps 0.625 NEAR, and 0.375 NEAR goes to maintenance. Every transaction is real and linked in the demo log.

Demo accounts are labelled as demo accounts. This is a testnet demonstration, not real world income.

What it is not

Not a savings circle. Not a crowdfunding page. Not a generic DAO treasury. It is the full flow from acquisition to revenue to distribution, with the maths checked onchain by a keyless verify script anyone can run.

Testnet only. No mainnet parts. No fee.

OwnGround runs on NEAR testnet. All figures shown are real onchain state, never simulated.
